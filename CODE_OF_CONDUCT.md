# Code of Conduct

Foundry Pay is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and
harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people;
- give specific, actionable, and proportionate feedback;
- respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and
  communication styles;
- disclose conflicts of interest and uncertainty;
- protect private security reports, credentials, and customer information;
- accept maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to disagree
  constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized conduct;
- personal insults, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption;
- publishing private information without permission;
- pressuring others to reveal secrets or bypass safety controls;
- fabricating evidence, hiding provenance, or misrepresenting review status;
- using project spaces primarily for spam or unrelated promotion.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their
verified GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue when a report contains
personal, security-sensitive, or identifying information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban
participants when necessary to protect the project and community. Decisions
should consider context, impact, recurrence, and willingness to repair harm.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents
the project.
